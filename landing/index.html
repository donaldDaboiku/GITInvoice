Privacy

Your business data stays on your device

GITInvoice is local-first. Invoices, customers, products, and settings live in your browser. Only license activation and buyer verification are sent to our servers.

  • Invoices and customers are stored locally — not in our database
  • Works offline after the first load
  • Export and import your data anytime as a JSON backup
  • PBKDF2 password hashing for multi-user accounts
