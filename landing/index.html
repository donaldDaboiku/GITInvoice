Privacy first

Your business data never leaves your device

GITInvoice is local-first. Invoices, customers, products, and settings are stored in your browser. Only license activation and buyer verification are sent securely to our servers.

  • 🔒 Invoices & customers stored locally — not in our database
  • 📡 Full functionality offline after first load
  • 💾 Export and import your data anytime as JSON backup
  • 🛡️ PBKDF2 password hashing for multi-user accounts
